“I would highly recommend DOT tot! My children currently attend here and have for the past 2+ years. Finding childcare initially was such a difficult task. I am a “planner” and had toured many places and got on waitlists early at the time. Initially I didn’t think dot tot would be in my top choices because we do not live near or work downtown so location wise it was not ideal and even today is quite far but so worth it for both the overall cost and quality care! I remember with many tours liking some other facilities that felt big and new, because dot tot is an older building in general, but when I asked about the more important aspects, like teacher retention and years of service, some of the “newer” places had major red flags, like teachers that have all only been there a year, or the most seasoned person being there “3 years” while dot tot blew me away- they have 2 teachers that have been there since the 80’s! The director is very proactive and has been there 20+ years and most other staff have been going on at least 3-5+ years now. This really speaks to the culture of the place and that they treat each other well and care about the work they do. I have also seen over time with my little ones and the various classrooms how helpful they are across all the rooms and with each other as an overall center. This gave me such great peace of mind compared to considering either a nanny at home that would have no accountability from someone else around, or larger centers that seemed like each class was their own “island” with little support from other teachers. Logistically, they also provide ALL the wipes, diapers, and milk [once they’re past breastmilk/formula] meals and snacks. This makes packing up SO much easier each day, and that being included into the cost is amazing. It is still one of the most affordable places somehow, likely because it is a non-profit! It also has a board of directors run by current parents so participation is really key and helpful and has kept up a culture of people that truly care about the kids, the environment they’re learning in, and the teachers well-being too. I may come to later regret this review if I eventually have another baby and then there’s a longer waitlist now for this awesome center, but your little ones would be in very caring hands here! It is never easy to hand over your most precious baby to anyone, but these gals are amazing in the daily work they do- they are so calm, patient and kind with them and truly care for the children they look after.”
