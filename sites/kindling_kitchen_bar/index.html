Our Story

Built around the hearth

Kindling started with a custom-built wood oven and a conviction: food tastes better when you let fire do the talking. Our kitchen works with farms within 60 miles, adjusting the menu weekly to follow what grows.

Every dish is cooked over hardwood coals, finished with smoke and char, and served in a dining room designed to feel like a friend's house — if your friend had impeccable taste and a 900-degree oven.
