“Great food and nice ambiance with a view overlooking a lake. If I were to be honest, the atmosphere could be enhanced with some ambient music and better lighting in the dining area. There’s a mixture of lighting that doesn’t flow well. The flow of the restaurant is not as appealing either. Also, you can see the highway as you look at the view of the lake from the dining area. Sunsets are nice but you see cars whizzing past too. The food is really very good, but does have room for improvement when it comes to the amount of salt and oil used for the octopus, and pork belly - too much. The beet salad was really good, but did have too much sauce. And the service, OMG, that’s the one thing that REALLY needs to improve. We did not appreciate the wait time for the server to take our order or after we received our order. She did not ever come back to ask if we needed more drinks or even dessert afterwards. And then we had to walk up to the bar to get our check because we saw the bartender chatting it up with 2 of the servers for over 15mins without bothering to see if we needed anything. And we noticed other patrons at the restaurant having the same unpleasant experience. Other than this, may be worth it to get some other dishes that could be cooked right. Worth trying once at least. Who knows, you may get better service and food 🤷🏽‍♀️”

SR

“Wow - what a gem. We had brunch, and were not expecting for this lake-side restaurant to be a little wonder. We started with cocktails and coffee because it was that type of morning. The coffee was rich….deep… delicious. When we sipped our first sip of the cocktails, we knew this spot cared about their craft. The bartender mixed the drinks to be strong but smooth; the way we like it. The food was delicious. The grilled chicken wrap was on point, and the huevos rancheros was too. We decided to get a dessert because we were so impressed with the food - the bread pudding did not disappoint. The service was elite - you don’t see many places anymore that provide the level of care of fine dining in a casual atmosphere. Obviously, the staff is well selected and trained. The whole experience led to an amazing brunch for my wife and me. We will definitely return.”
